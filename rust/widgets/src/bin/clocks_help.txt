Clocks: this server's, everyone else's, and the ones counting down.

A big clock in the machine's own timezone, countdown bars for the next hour,
office hours and the end of day, and a world clock covering the hubs you care
about.

    clocks

The world clock marks each city with a sun or a moon by its local hour, which
is the fastest way to read whether it is a reasonable time to message someone
there, and shows the day offset where the date differs from this machine's.

Timezones come from the IANA database, so the offsets are right across a
daylight-saving boundary rather than fixed at whatever they were when the
config was written.

Keys: up/down scroll the cities, q quits.
