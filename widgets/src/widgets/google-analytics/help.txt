Google Analytics 4: is traffic normal right now, and did the last change hurt key events?

GA's own screens say how many people are on the site, not whether that is
normal for this minute of a Tuesday, or whether key events fell after this
morning's deploy. Each section here answers one question you would act on:
live users against a baseline this widget records itself, today against the
same weekday, key events to the same hour, the pages Google Search sends
visitors to, and the pages and channels that moved.

    google-analytics

Signs in with a Google service-account key (credentials_file), which mints
its own hour-long token indefinitely, or with a pasted token for an hour of
testing (token, or $GOOGLE_OAUTH_ACCESS_TOKEN), which the screen says will
expire. Setup on Google's side - one Cloud project, three APIs, the service
account as Viewer on each property - is docs/google-setup.md.

Realtime is read every 15 s by default, backing off to 60 s after five
minutes with nobody on the site and slowing to 120 s when under a tenth of
the property's quota is left; the header says which. Processed reports come
every 5 minutes and run 2-6 hours behind, so an hour GA has not processed is
drawn hatched, never as zero. Every request reads GA's property quota back.

The baseline is the median of active users at this minute of the week over
the last 4 weeks, from samples this widget wrote to
$XDG_STATE_HOME/opscope/google-analytics/. It uses only weeks the widget was
running at that minute and says how many that was; with none it says so
rather than showing a figure.

Keys: up/down move between rows, tab jumps to the next section, left/right
change property, Enter opens a page, key event or property in detail and esc
closes it, m cycles the metric, w cycles the window, s cycles the sort, /
filters by text, l cycles the realtime interval, c copies the property's GA
URL, r refreshes now, comma opens settings, q quits.

ctrl-y and ctrl-e scroll the view a line at a time, as in vim, and the
mouse wheel does the same. They move the pane and never the selection,
which is what the arrows are for.
