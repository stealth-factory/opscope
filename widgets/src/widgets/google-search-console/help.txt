Google Search Console: what moved in Google Search, and what is broken in the index.

Shows the last 24 hours of search clicks by the hour, the pages and queries
that moved against the previous window, the search terms that bring the
most clicks and the page each lands on, URL Inspection of the pages that
earn the traffic, and whether the sitemaps are still being read.

    google-search-console

Every figure is Google's and says how far behind Google keeps it: hourly
data is a few hours behind and the latest hours are still being collected
(drawn hatched, never as a drop); final data is two or three days behind.
Hours come from Google in Pacific time and are drawn in local time.

Keys: up/down select a row, tab moves to the next section, left/right
switch property when there is more than one, and Enter opens the selected
row - 28 days of a page or query with its top queries or pages, the full
inspection of a page, the pages a search term lands on, a sitemap's
contents, or a property's permission and budget. m cycles the movers
metric (impressions, clicks, CTR, position). w toggles the window between
7 and 28 days. d toggles final and fresh data. t cycles the search type
(web, image, video, news, discover). press i to inspect the selected page
now, which spends one of the property's 2,000 daily inspections; Enter
never does. / filters by text and esc clears it, c copies the selected row,
r refreshes, comma opens settings, q quits. In a detail view, l copies the
page's Search Console link, and esc or left closes it.

Inspection is bounded: the top inspect_top pages by impressions (25 by
default) and as many that dropped to zero, each once a day, cached for 14
days under $XDG_STATE_HOME/opscope/google-search-console/. Search Analytics
ranks pages by clicks and returns at most 1,000; when that cut the answer
the header says the impressions ranking is among those. The Page
Indexing report has no API, so the INDEX HEALTH section is that sample and
says so.

Sign-in: a service-account key file in google_search_console.credentials_file,
shared with google-analytics, or a pasted token in $GOOGLE_OAUTH_ACCESS_TOKEN
for an hour of testing. docs/google-setup.md walks through the Google side.

ctrl-y and ctrl-e scroll the view a line at a time, as in vim, and the
mouse wheel does the same. They move the pane and never the selection,
which is what the arrows are for.
